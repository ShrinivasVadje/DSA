My sad Life

My life
is not loud with pain,
it is silent—
like a room after everyone leaves.
I wake up every day
with dreams folded carefully inside me,
but the world opens them roughly
and asks me to be strong again.
People see my smile
and think I am winning,
they don’t hear
the tired conversations
I have with myself at 2 a.m.
I try,
I really do.
I run, I learn, I fight,
but sometimes my heart whispers,
“How long?”
I carry expectations
like stones in my pocket—
family, future, society,
all pulling me downward
while I pretend I can swim.
